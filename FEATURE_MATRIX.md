## Where Booksane can win

Nobody does all of these together:

1. **Vellum quality on every computer.** Vellum is Mac only; Atticus is everywhere but is not known for Vellum-level layout.
2. **Writing, design and publishing in one place**, without Scrivener's learning curve.
3. **Proof that the file is right.** A clear check of every export (fonts, images, page count, store rules). No competitor shows this.
4. **Free to start, fair price after.** Reedsy is free but basic; Vellum is $250.

## What to build, in order

**1. Design (the visible gap):** 12+ themes, chapter heading styles, drop caps, first-line small caps, ornamental scene breaks, chapter images, more fonts, all common trim sizes, large print, callouts.

**2. Ebook parity:** device preview, Kindle and store-specific files, store links, automatic "also by" pages, image compression.

**3. Writing tools:** drag-and-drop, parts, goals and streaks, find and replace, focus mode, footnotes.

**4. Print pro:** bleed, PDF/X, hardcover, spine and cover size calculator, balanced spreads.

**5. Growth:** box sets, review copies, DOCX export, cover maker, accounts and cloud sync, collaboration.
